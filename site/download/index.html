The Board RPG player

Plays games made with the editor: open a .brpg file with it (a double-click once it's installed), or drop one onto its window.

Looking for the latest version…
The builds aren't signed yet. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" – choose More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. On Linux, make the AppImage executable (chmod +x) and run it.

Ship your own game

Put your exported game next to the player as game.brpg – the player then starts it right away. Zip the two together and you have a finished game to hand out.
